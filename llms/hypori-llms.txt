# Hypori

> Hypori empowers secure virtual access to enterprise apps and data from any mobile device with total personal privacy. Hypori is a Virginia-based technology company providing Virtual Mobile Infrastructure (VMI) and zero-trust mobile access. Stream pixels, not data. Tagline: One Device, Zero Worries®.

## Products

- [Hypori Mobile](https://www.hypori.com/hyporimobile) - Secure virtual workspace for BYOD and zero-trust access; the physical device streams encrypted pixels only, with no organizational data stored on the endpoint.
- [Hypori Lyte](https://www.hypori.com/lyte) - Lightweight VMI with three variants: Lyte for Secure Messaging, Lyte for Apps, and Lyte for Enterprise Browser—single-purpose delivery without a full workspace.
- [Hypori Secure Messaging](https://www.hypori.com/securemessaging) - Private, compliant mobile messaging; messages and attachments stay inside a controlled virtual environment, never on the personal device.

## Solutions

- [Secure BYOD](https://www.hypori.com/secure-byod) - Worry-free BYOD with total personal privacy; no intrusive MDM or MAM software, and no risk of personal device wipe or confiscation.
- [Secure Work](https://www.hypori.com/securework) - Secure remote work via pixel streaming; Hypori never processes, stores, or transmits data on the personal device.
- [Secure Mobile Access](https://www.hypori.com/secure-mobile-access) - Secure, zero-trust mobile access for enterprise and regulated workforces.
- [CMMC Compliance](https://www.hypori.com/cmmc) - Keep mobile endpoints out of CMMC Level 2 assessment scope when CUI and FCI never reach the device.
- [Pricing](https://www.hypori.com/pricing) - Customized, scalable pricing for secure, worry-free BYOD—from small teams to large enterprise deployments.

## Compliance & Security

Hypori is built on a zero-trust architecture trusted by defense, government, healthcare, and other regulated industries. Certifications and authorizations cited on the website include FedRAMP High, DoW Impact Level 5 (IL5), NIAP Common Criteria, NSA Commercial Solutions for Classified (CSfC), and SOC 2 Type II. Hypori keeps CUI, FCI, PII, and PHI off the endpoint entirely — no data ever touches the device. That reduces both attack surface and compliance scope under CMMC 2.0 Level 2, DFARS 252.204-7012, HIPAA, and NIST 800-171 for remote and BYOD users, and satisfies the No TikTok on Government Devices Act by design.
